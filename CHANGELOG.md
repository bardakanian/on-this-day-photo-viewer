# Changelog

With every change, bump `VERSION` in `onthisday/core/constants.py` and add an entry here.

## 1.18.0 — 2026-11-21

- The gallery is a grid of square tiles, like Photos: each picture fills its tile, with no filename, folder or buttons in the way. **⌘=** and **⌘−** zoom (so do ⌘-scroll and pinch), **⌘0** goes back to the default, and the size is remembered. Right-click a tile for Open and Move to Trash; hovering shows its name and time.
- Previews are sharper. They keep the whole picture instead of padding it into a fixed box, and **Settings > Preview quality** chooses High (1024 px, the default) or Standard (512 px). The first scan after this update remakes every saved preview in the new format, which takes a while for a large library.
- **Video Library** moved from the toolbar to **Settings > Video Library…**; **⌘⇧V** still opens it.
- Settings no longer opens too short to show the explanations under each setting, and on a small screen or with a large system font it scrolls instead of pushing Save and Cancel off the bottom.
- The Media by year chart in Library Insights fills its card instead of stopping half way across.
- The viewer's photo zoom keys (**⌘=**, **⌘−**, **⌘0**) work again; the new tile-size shortcuts no longer take them while the viewer is open.
- README screenshots are regenerated by `tools/make_screenshots.py`, which now also captures the viewer and Settings.

## 1.17.1 — 2026-11-17

- After a scan, the gallery and the Video Library now refresh when only the grouping of Live Photos changed. Before, they refreshed only if a file was added, changed or removed, so a window could keep showing a Live Photo and its clip separately until you changed the day or restarted the app.

## 1.17.0 — 2026-11-15

- While you scroll through a year in the gallery, its heading (the year and its item count) stays pinned at the top. Click it to collapse that year without scrolling back up; the gallery then jumps to the collapsed year, so you don't lose your place. The next year's heading pushes it up as it arrives.

## 1.16.0 — 2026-11-15

- A gear button at the right end of the toolbar opens **Settings**. Before, Settings was only in the menu bar (⌘,). The gear follows the light or dark theme.

## 1.15.0 — 2026-11-15

- New setting, **Settings > Check the library for changes when I return to the app** (on by default). Turn it off and the app never scans the drive on its own: not when you come back to it after 10 minutes, and not to look for a disconnected drive. The library is then only checked when you choose **Rescan**.

## 1.14.0 — 2026-11-15

- Settings shows how much space the saved previews take ("Saved previews: 4.2 GB (119,530 files)"), counted in the background so the dialog opens at once.
- **Delete All Previews…** in Settings frees that space. It asks first and says what you lose: without previews, the gallery can't show the library while its drive is disconnected. If saving previews is still on, they're saved again in the background.
- Turning off **Save a preview of every photo and video** now offers to delete the previews already saved. Before, it only stopped new ones, and the old ones stayed on disk.
- Scans now delete the previews of Live Photo clips, which aren't shown on their own.

## 1.13.0 — 2026-11-15

- Live Photos: an iPhone Live Photo saved as two files (for example `IMG_4330.HEIC` and `IMG_4330.MOV`) now shows once, as a photo with a Live Photo badge, instead of a photo plus a separate 1–3 second video. The two files are paired when they share a folder and a name and were taken within 5 seconds of each other.
- In the viewer, **◉ LIVE** (or L) plays the motion and then returns to the still. Press it again, or Space, to stop early.
- Moving a Live Photo to the Trash moves its video too. If the video can't be moved, it stays in the library as a video of its own.
- The Video Library, the Videos filter and Library Insights no longer count Live Photo clips as videos. Existing indexes are paired the first time the app opens, with no rescan.

## 1.12.0 — 2026-11-13

- Scans and saving previews show how far along they are and about how long is left ("12,340 of about 111,000 checked · about 2 min left"), with a progress bar that fills up under the toolbar. The estimate counts down steadily: it's based on the last minute's pace blended with the pace since the start, so bursts of quick files and stalls on slow ones (HEIC photos, videos) don't make it bounce.
- When they finish, the status bar says so and how long it took ("scan finished in 1 min", "all previews saved (91,000 new, in 3 h)").
- Jobs that took more than a minute also show a system notification when they finish (Notification Center on macOS, a tray message on Windows). If you've switched to another app, the Dock icon bounces too (the taskbar button flashes on Windows).
- A first scan, whose total isn't known yet, says so instead of guessing.

## 1.11.0 — 2026-11-12

- Times show on a 12-hour clock (2:05 PM) in the viewer, its Info panel, the gallery, and the Video Library. **Settings > Time format** switches back to 24-hour (14:05).

## 1.10.3 — 2026-11-12

- Fixed the window freezing for up to two seconds. The stall log showed the whole theme being re-applied again and again while the app was open; it is now redone only when light or dark really changes, and Settings no longer restyles the app when you save without changing the theme.
- The gallery no longer asks the file system for the library's path every time it draws a card, which cost about a third of a second per redraw on Windows.
- The log file is written in UTF-8, so file names and the "·" in log lines are no longer garbled.

## 1.10.2 — 2026-11-12

- The app now writes a line to its log whenever the window stops responding for more than 0.2 seconds, with how long it lasted, what the app was doing (page, scan, saving previews) and where in the code it was stuck. This is to find the cause of stutter. It looks at a stall only after it ends, adds no delay, and keeps at most 300 entries per session.

## 1.10.1 — 2026-11-08

- On a read-only drive, such as an NTFS drive on a Mac, Move to Trash is dimmed and explains why, instead of asking for confirmation and then failing with a permission error.

## 1.10.0 — 2026-11-08

- After each scan, a preview of every item is saved in the background, starting with the calendar days coming up soonest. The status bar shows the progress. It can be turned off in Settings.
- While the library's drive is disconnected, the gallery still shows every indexed item with its saved preview instead of an empty page. The viewer shows the preview enlarged to fit, and Open in Default App, Move to Trash and video playback wait until the drive is back. The Info panel falls back to the date in the index.
- Saving previews resumes when the app opens, without scanning the library.
- The viewer keeps a photo's preview on screen when the original can't be read, instead of replacing it with an error.

## 1.9.1 — 2026-11-08

- The app no longer scans the library every time it opens; it shows the saved index right away. Use **Rescan** to pick up new files at any time. The library is still re-checked when you return to the app after 10 minutes.

## 1.7.0 — 2026-11-02

- The library refreshes itself: at launch, and when you return to the app after 10 minutes. Scans that find nothing new leave the gallery as it is.
- A library on a drive that isn't connected is no longer forgotten. The gallery says the folder is unavailable, and the photos come back as soon as the drive does. Before, changing a setting while the drive was unplugged erased the saved folder.
- The gallery is a virtual grid: only the cards on screen are drawn and load thumbnails, so every item shows without "Load more" and scrolling stays smooth at any size. Right-click a card for Open and Move to Trash.
- Videos keep the local time they were recorded at (QuickTime creation date) instead of converting UTC with this computer's time zone, so travel videos land on the right day. The next scan re-reads dates once.
- `tools/build_app.py` builds a standalone app (macOS .app, Windows .exe) that runs without Python or the project folder.
- GitHub Actions runs the tests on macOS and Windows and builds both apps for every push.
- Fixed the empty-gallery message being cut off at the bottom.

## 1.6.0 — 2026-11-01

- Info panel in the viewer: select **Info** or press **I** to see the capture date and where it came from, dimensions, type, file size and path. Videos also show duration, frame rate and codec.

## 1.5.1 — 2026-11-01

- Photos are dated by when they were taken (EXIF DateTimeOriginal). Before, that tag was never found, so edited photos got their edit date. The next scan re-reads every date once.
- A date that is a placeholder (1904, 1970, 1980) no longer stops the search; the next date in the photo or video is tried.
- Thumbnails are written to a temporary file and renamed into place, so a half-written one is never shown or kept.
- Hidden and system files are no longer indexed: macOS "._" files on external and network drives, `.Trashes`, Synology `@eaDir`, and Windows `$RECYCLE.BIN`.
- February 29 photos and videos show on February 28 in years without a leap day.
- After a delete, the gallery keeps the years you expanded with "Load more" or collapsed, and no longer jumps later.
- The Video Library count respects an active search after Refresh.

## 1.5.0 — 2026-11-01

- Show the version number in the window title ("On This Day 1.5.0") and in the macOS launcher's Get Info.
- Added this changelog.

## 1.4.1 — 2026-10-31

- Fixed the viewer going black when a photo finished loading after "Loading…" was shown (for example after jumping with End).

## 1.4.0 — 2026-10-28

- `tools/make_launcher.py` adds a Start-menu (and optional Desktop) shortcut on Windows.
- README documents the in-app viewer and its keyboard shortcuts.

## 1.3.1 — 2026-10-28

- Files without capture metadata are no longer dated by when they were copied; placeholder dates (1904, 1970, 1980) are ignored.

## 1.3.0 — 2026-10-28

- In-app viewer for photos and videos: zoom and pan, previous/next, full-resolution decoding on zoom, prefetching of neighbours, and Move to Trash.
- Gallery queries and thumbnail reads run in the background, and indexing reads capture dates in parallel.
- macOS launcher in the Applications folder (`tools/make_launcher.py`).

## 1.2.0 — 2026-10-28

- Sharper Retina thumbnails, faster JPEG decoding, and a fallback frame for videos that fail to seek.
- Files that can't be previewed are remembered, so they aren't retried on every scroll.
